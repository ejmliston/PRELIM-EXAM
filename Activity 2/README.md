# ☕ Activity 2 – Brand Identity Design: Sunday Brew

## 📌 Overview

This activity focuses on the development of a **brand identity and visual design system** for a fictional coffee brand called **Sunday Brew**.

The activity demonstrates the application of design principles such as **logo development, color selection, typography, visual consistency, and brand presentation**. The goal is to create a warm, welcoming, and memorable identity that reflects the relaxing experience associated with enjoying coffee on a Sunday.

---

## ☕ Brand Name

# Sunday Brew

### Tagline
> **Your Perfect Sunday, Every Day**

The name **Sunday Brew** represents a relaxing and comforting coffee experience. The concept is inspired by the feeling of having a warm cup of coffee during a peaceful Sunday.

---

## 🎨 Brand Logo

The Sunday Brew logo features a stylized **sunrise/sunset-inspired design combined with a coffee bean or coffee cup element**.

The design uses curved shapes and warm colors to communicate:

- ☀️ Warmth
- ☕ Coffee and comfort
- 🌅 Relaxation
- 🧡 Positivity
- 🌈 A welcoming atmosphere

The logo was designed to be simple, recognizable, and suitable for different branding applications.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

The visual identity uses a warm and inviting color palette.

| Color | Hex Code | Purpose |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 🟠 Orange | `#E35619` | Energy, warmth, and coffee |
| ⚫ Black | `#000000` | Contrast, elegance, and readability |
| 🟡 Golden Yellow | `#FFA51F` | Warmth, positivity, and sunlight |
| 🤍 Soft Cream | `#FBFEF9` | Cleanliness, simplicity, and comfort |

### Color Concept

The combination of orange, yellow, black, and cream creates a **cozy and energetic coffee-shop aesthetic** while maintaining a clean and professional appearance.

---

## ✍️ Typography

Two primary typefaces were selected for the brand:

### 1. Noto Serif Condensed

Used mainly for:

- Headings
- Brand name
- Information text
- Formal content

This typeface provides a classic and sophisticated appearance.

### 2. Magnolia Script

Used mainly for:

- Taglines
- Decorative text
- Accent elements

The script font adds a more personal, elegant, and inviting feel to the brand.

---

## 💡 Design Concept

The overall concept of **Sunday Brew** revolves around the idea of making every day feel like a relaxing Sunday.

The warm orange and yellow tones represent **sunlight, energy, and warmth**, while the cream background creates a sense of **comfort and relaxation**. Black is used to provide contrast and improve readability.

The combination of serif and script typography creates a balance between **professionalism and personality**.
